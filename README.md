### Tesla Digital Transformation Strategy

An independent academic case study of how Tesla uses software, connected vehicles, AI and energy technology,
with three proposals for how the company could grow:

1. **Autonomous mobility platform:** a Robotaxi service with fleet operations and recurring revenue.
2. **Vehicle-to-grid energy:** letting compatible cars share stored energy with the grid, alongside Tesla's solar
   and battery products.
3. **AI data and software platform:** predictive maintenance, software subscriptions and developer services.

The analysis uses the Digital Maturity Model, Porter's Five Forces, platform theory and the Business Model Canvas,
and covers revenue models, required skills, risks and regulation.

[Read the report (PDF)](digital-transformation/tesla-strategy/Tesla_Digital_Transformation_Report.pdf) ·
[View the presentation (PDF)](digital-transformation/tesla-strategy/Tesla_Digital_Transformation_Presentation.pdf)

*This is academic analysis, not work done for Tesla, and the proposals are not services Tesla has adopted.*
