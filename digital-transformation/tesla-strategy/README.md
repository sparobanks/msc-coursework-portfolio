# Tesla Digital Transformation Strategy

An MSc Computer Science and Technology coursework case study for the Digital Transformation module at Ulster University. This is an independent academic analysis of Tesla, Inc., not a project completed for Tesla.

## Overview

I analysed how Tesla uses software, connected vehicles, AI, data and energy technology. I then proposed three ways the company could develop its business:

1. **Autonomous mobility platform:** A proposed Robotaxi service with fleet operations and recurring revenue.
2. **Vehicle-to-grid energy ecosystem:** A proposed way for compatible vehicles to share stored energy with the grid alongside Tesla’s solar and storage products.
3. **AI-driven data and software platform:** Proposed predictive maintenance, software subscriptions and developer services.

These are strategic proposals, not systems I built or services Tesla has adopted.

## What I did

- Reviewed Tesla’s digital capabilities, business model and competition in the EV industry.
- Applied the Digital Maturity Model, Porter’s Five Forces, platform theory and the Business Model Canvas.
- Mapped the proposed revenue models, customer groups, process changes and skills needed for each initiative.
- Examined risks involving safety, regulation, supply chains and implementation.
- Created a report with diagrams covering the proposed platform, autonomous driving data workflow and vehicle-to-grid manufacturing changes, plus a presentation introducing the case study.
